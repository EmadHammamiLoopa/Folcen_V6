Welcome to Folcen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

Welcome to Folcen, a mobile application developed by Emad Hammami that operates as a hybrid chat and social platform. This Privacy Policy explains how Folcen collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal information, and describes your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws. By using our Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Folcen collects various types of information to provide and improve our services. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Information provided directly by users such as name, email, date of birth, address, and other identifiers necessary for account creation and personalization.
  • Log Data: Automatically collected information when you use our Service, including your device's Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, app configuration, usage statistics, and timestamps of interactions.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, personalize, and improve our services.
  • To communicate with you, including notifications about updates and new features.
  • To analyze and optimize app performance, troubleshoot issues, and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

We process personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: When you provide us with explicit consent to process your personal information.
  • Contractual Necessity: When processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you (e.g., providing requested services).
  • Legitimate Interests: When processing is necessary for our legitimate interests and does not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Legal Obligations: When processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Folcen may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, improve our services, and analyze usage patterns. You have the option to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our Service.

Third-Party Data Processors

To operate Folcen, we rely on the following third-party services that may process your personal data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC, USA) — used for user authentication, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and crash reporting. Data may be transferred to the USA under Standard Contractual Clauses. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas (MongoDB Inc., USA) — our cloud database where your account data, messages, and posts are stored. Protected under EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See MongoDB Privacy Policy.
  • PeerJS / WebRTC — used for peer-to-peer audio and video calls. Audio/video streams are transmitted directly between devices and are NOT stored on our servers. Signalling metadata (call initiation/termination) is processed temporarily on our servers.
  • Socket.IO — used for real-time messaging and presence (online/offline status). Connection data is ephemeral and not persisted beyond your session.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Data Sharing and Legal Compliance

We may share your personal information in the following circumstances:

  • Legal Obligations: To comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request from public authorities.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Folcen, our users, or the public.

Security Measures

Folcen implements industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

Children’s Privacy

Folcen does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 18. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will promptly delete it from our servers.

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccuracies in your personal information.
  • Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to request that we delete your personal information. You can delete your account at any time in the app settings, and we will permanently remove your data within 30 days.
  • Restriction: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Data Portability: You have the right to receive a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly-used, machine-readable format. Use the data export feature in the app or contact us.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: If we rely on your consent to process your personal information, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your residence, if you believe that our processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at folcenteam@gmail.com or use the account settings within the app. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Account data (name, email, profile): retained until you delete your account, then permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Messages: retained while your account is active; permanently deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Channel posts: may be anonymised (author removed) upon account deletion to preserve community context.
  • Server logs: retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes.

Local Storage and Device Data

Folcen stores certain data on your device for performance and offline functionality, including your authentication token, cached profile data, and notification preferences. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you log out or uninstall the app.
